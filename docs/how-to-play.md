# How to play

## Start

Run `python3 -m http.server` in the project folder and open `localhost:8000`.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move | Arrow keys or W A S D |
| Confirm (advance dialogue, read, pick an answer) | **Z** or Enter |
| Cancel (leave a puzzle or the notebook) | X or Esc |
| Notebook (open and close, also inside a puzzle) | **C**, or Tab while typing |
| Erase a typed letter | Backspace |

The game opens on a prologue. Arrow keys do nothing there. A small box above the dialogue shows **Z: continuar**. Press Z or Enter to read through it, then you can walk.

## The garden

- The character in the white robe is you. The game starts at the west end of the Garden of Eden.
- A river runs along the top and splits into four branches that cut the garden into four regions. The path crosses each branch where a wooden bridge is built, plank by plank, once the region's puzzle is solved.
- Grey stones hold verses. Stand next to one and press Z to read it. What you read goes into the notebook.
- The seven day stones of the first region hide their verse behind a blank. Each one names a passage of Gênesis: look it up in your own Bible, type the missing word and press Enter. A filled stone lights up and its verse goes into the notebook. When all seven are lit, the pedestals light up and the bridge appears.
- A landmark at the end of the other regions gives its puzzle: the large stone, the tree in the middle of the garden, the cracked stone. Some puzzles only start after you read the stones they need.
- Standing next to a stone or landmark shows a small box at the bottom right with the button to press. Puzzles show their buttons the same way.
- At the east end, cherubim and a flaming sword guard the way. They let you through once the four torn pages are put back in order.

## Puzzles

1. Stand next to a landmark and press Z. Read the dialogue (Z) until the puzzle appears.
2. List puzzles: move the `>` cursor with Up and Down and press Z on an answer. Some ask for the right order, and the picked items show above the list as "Montado". Others ask you to mark every item that fits, in any order.
3. Blanks: type a word (accents and case do not matter) and press Enter. Letters go into the answer, so use Tab for the notebook and Esc to leave.
4. A wrong answer shows "Não é isso.". There is no game over, try again.
5. Each region puzzle gives a torn page and opens the next crossing. Press X to leave a puzzle without solving it.

The notebook (C) shows your torn pages, the books already rebuilt in the Bible, and every stone you read.
